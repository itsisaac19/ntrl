Introducing 'NTRL' – the app that connects the world in a single question. Every day, discover questions that reach deeper than than just the surface. Answer during the sunrise and sunset, allow yourself to enjoy nature. Join a broader community, celebrating shared experiences and sparking meaningful conversations. Ntrl – where the world's beauty and your deepest thoughts come together. Join the conversation today. 

This project was submitted to the 2023 Congressional App Challenge. Watch the video here: [youtube link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qjYsQ3QRgyw)
